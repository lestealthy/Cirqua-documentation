## How claims are attributed

Three claim types appear across this documentation set and they are never
blurred together:

* **Project fact** — reproduced from `https://cirqua-water.eu/` or
  `https://www.african-biotech.com/`, with a citation on the
  <a href="../references/official-project-sources.html">Official Project Sources</a>
  page.
* **Firmware implementation** — read directly from the audited source at commit
  `db6d9b8`, with a file, symbol and line reference.
* **Engineering interpretation** — the author's reading of the two above. Always
  labelled as such on the page where it appears.

!!! note
    Project-level claims are only reproduced as far as the official site states
    them. The official site was consulted on 2026-10-03. Do not extend budget,
    duration or consortium figures beyond that without re-checking the source.

## Relationship to the organisation pages

* **CIRQUA** — the funded research project. Owns project-level claims.
* **African Biotechnology Company (ABC)** — CIRQUA partner, based in Tunis,
  Tunisia. Owns its own marks and service descriptions.
* **WattLab** — authored and maintains this documentation and the firmware.

No organisation owns another's marks, and nothing on these pages grants
permission to reuse any mark or logo.
